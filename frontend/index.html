    How to use

    • Symbol Lookup: type a company name or ticker and pick a suggestion. Candles load when you open Price & Candles.
    • Market Movers: gainers, losers and most active names for India, USA and more. Click a row to open it in Symbol Lookup.
    • Screener: filter by price, change, volume and market cap. Presets give you a quick start.
    • Tables: click a column heading to sort, use the filter box to narrow rows, and download as CSV or JSON.
    • Shortcuts: press / to focus the symbol box, 1 2 3 to switch sections.
