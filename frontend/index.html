New to the market? Three things worth knowing

  1. You can just type a name. Every stock has a code like NSE:RELIANCE, but you never need to remember it. Search "Reliance" and we find it.
  2. Free prices are usually 15 minutes late. Look for the Delayed tag. Crypto shows Live.
  3. Amounts use the stock's own money. Indian companies are in rupees, written in lakh and crore. Tap any number to see what it means.

Type the name of a company, a ticker or a coin. You get today's price, what it means in plain words, a price chart and the company's key numbers.
